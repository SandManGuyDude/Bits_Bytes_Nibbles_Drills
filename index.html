myInt Drills

Practice editing x.value through bytes, nibbles and bits.

Cheat sheet
  • byte0 is the RIGHTMOST pair of hex digits, byte3 is the leftmost (little-endian).
  • In each byte, nibble0 is the right digit, nibble1 is the left digit.
  • bit0–bit3 are in the right digit (worth 1, 2, 4, 8). bit4–bit7 are in the left digit (worth 1, 2, 4, 8 of that digit).
  • Fields keep only what fits: a nibble keeps 4 bits, a bit keeps 1.
